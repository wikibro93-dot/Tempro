### Social Media = Data Harvesting

Every time you sign up for Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok, or LinkedIn, your email can be tied to tracking, spam, targeted ads, and data broker profiles. A burner email gives you a practical layer of protection without turning your main inbox into a marketing channel.

### Why a Burner Email Helps

- **Privacy protection** – you can hide your main address from platforms that collect contact data.
- **Spam control** – keep promotional emails and account notices out of your personal inbox.
- **Account separation** – use a different address for each platform or signup flow.
- **Low risk** – you can delete the account later without disrupting your everyday inbox.

### Best Practices for Social Signup Privacy

1. Use a fresh burner email for each platform if you want the cleanest separation.
2. Avoid reusing the same address for personal communications and social signups.
3. For long-term accounts, consider a private domain or a more stable alias instead of a public temp mailbox.
4. Pair your burner email with a password manager so you can still recover accounts when needed.

### When a Burner Email Is Not Enough

Some platforms will still ask for a real phone number, identity proof, or a stable recovery address. If you need ongoing access to a long-term profile, a private domain or dedicated alias is usually the better long-term option.
