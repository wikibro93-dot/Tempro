Expired Temp Inbox: What Happens, How to Delete, and How to Choose Expiry Next Time

An expired temporary inbox cannot be extended or renewed: its messages are assumed unrecoverable, so create a new address and choose a longer expiry at creation next time.

Published on 2026-10-29 by TempMail Pro Team

Expiry is the point of a temporary inbox. Without it, a disposable address would slowly become a permanent one. But expiry still catches people by surprise, usually at the worst moment: a confirmation link you meant to save, or an account you forgot was tied to a temporary address. This article explains what happens at expiry, what you can and cannot do afterwards, and how to choose better next time.

What happens when an inbox expires

When a temporary inbox reaches the end of its set duration, it stops receiving new mail, and its existing messages are treated as gone. In TempMail Pro, expired inboxes and their messages are assumed unrecoverable. Do not treat expiry as a soft deadline or a folder you can reopen later. Treat it as a closing door.

There is also no working extend or renew option for an expired inbox in the app. You cannot bring an expired inbox back to life or recover a message from it by creating a similar address. A new address, even with the same custom name, is a different inbox, and mail sent to the old address after expiry will not be forwarded to it.

That is the privacy property of temporary mail: short-lived mail does not linger. You must save anything worth keeping before expiry.

What to do when you realise an inbox has expired

First, check the facts. Confirm whether the inbox is truly expired, or whether you are looking at the wrong inbox among several open ones.

If the inbox is genuinely expired, ask what you actually lost. A verification link you already used means nothing is lost. An unused code is gone, and you will need to request a fresh one using a new temporary address or a permanent address. An account tied only to that inbox is the serious case: if expiry has passed, you may need to contact the service's support, or accept that the account cannot be recovered through email.

Deleting an inbox you no longer need

Expiry is automatic, but deletion is a choice you can make earlier. When a signup, trial, or test is finished, delete its inbox rather than leaving it open until expiry. Deletion closes it immediately and clears it from your workspace.

In TempMail Pro, deletion is the action the app supports for an inbox you no longer need. Open the inbox, choose delete, and confirm. There is no need to keep an empty inbox "just in case": generating a fresh address later takes seconds.

Delete inboxes at the end of each task, while you still remember what each one was for.

Choosing the right expiry next time

Because expiry is set at creation time, and an expired inbox cannot be renewed, the most important decision happens before you generate the address. TempMail Pro offers 10 minutes, 1 hour, 24 hours, 7 days, or a custom duration:

| Task | A sensible expiry to choose at creation | |---|---| | A single verification code | 10 minutes, or 1 hour if the service is slow | | A short signup with a follow-up email | 1 hour, or 24 hours to be safe | | A trial or discussion lasting several days | 24 hours, or 7 days for a full week | | A project spanning more than a week | A custom duration covering the whole project |

Two mistakes recur: picking 10 minutes for a task that will take longer, and picking 7 days by default for a ten-minute job. A thirty-second pause before creation prevents both. If torn between two durations, pick the longer one, provided the task genuinely spans that period.

Save what matters before expiry

Save important items outside the inbox as soon as they arrive, and note each inbox's expiry date in your task list. Do not use a temporary inbox as storage; it is a transit point, not a filing cabinet.

Accounts that should never be temporary

Temporary inboxes are for accounts you can afford to lose. Do not use one for banking, payments, government services, medical accounts, work accounts, password recovery for a subscription you keep, or any account holding content you paid for. Those accounts need a permanent address from the start.

A short expiry routine

• [ ] Name the task and estimate how long it will really take.

• [ ] Choose an expiry at creation that covers that whole period.

• [ ] Give the inbox a custom name tied to the task.

• [ ] Save anything important outside the inbox as soon as it arrives.

• [ ] Delete the inbox when the task ends, rather than waiting for expiry.

• [ ] If the task grows into something permanent, move the account to a permanent address before expiry.

Handled this way, expiry becomes a planned ending: the inbox closes, the task is finished, and nothing important was left inside it. If an inbox does expire before you are ready, create a new address, choose a longer expiry at creation this time, and start again.
